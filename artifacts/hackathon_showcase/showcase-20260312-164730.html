Why This Bridge Is An Unlock

UnlockDesign SignalWhy It Matters
Model output -> policy gate, not just model output -> scoreModelBridge binds model_output + proof/model hashes + bounds + timestamp into a Groth16 public-signal commitment that policy lanes can enforce.Lets AI recommendations become cryptographically gateable execution intents.
